What I'm building now

I'm building a machine learning model that reads a dating conversation and catches red flags and green flags — and shows which messages led to each one, so the result can be checked rather than just trusted.

What it is

The platform uses Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to analyze dating conversations, identify potential green and red flags, and give meaningful insight into interpersonal communication.

It does not judge people or guess their intentions. It focuses on what can be observed: behaviors, communication patterns, consistency, emotional awareness and mutual respect.

Core features

🚩

Green & red flag detection

Identify potentially healthy and unhealthy relationship behaviors through contextual analysis.

💬

AI conversation analysis

Understand communication styles, engagement, reciprocity and interaction patterns.

🔁

Behavioral pattern recognition

Detect recurring patterns, inconsistencies, boundary violations and potential manipulation indicators.

🌱

Relationship insights

Personalized insights that encourage self-awareness and healthier relationship decisions.

🧭

Context-aware analysis

Consider conversational context and alternative interpretations rather than isolated messages.

🔍

Explainable AI

Transparent assessments backed by specific evidence from the conversation, confidence levels and explanations.

To make AI a tool for better human understanding, emotional awareness and healthier relationships.

Privacy & data use

  • Anonymous. No account and no name required. Please remove names, usernames, phone numbers, addresses, workplaces and anything else that could identify someone — including the other person.
  • Purpose. Submissions are used only to research, train and evaluate models that detect healthy and unhealthy communication patterns.
  • Open dataset. After review and further anonymization, submissions may be published as part of an open research dataset.
  • Storage. The form is processed by Formspree. This site has no tracking or analytics.
  • Removal. If you want something you submitted removed, open an issue on our GitHub with a short quote so we can find it.
  • Not advice. This project is research, not therapy or legal advice. If you feel unsafe in a relationship, contact local emergency services or a domestic-abuse helpline.
